Huygens' Principle & Slit Diffraction

Core Concept: Every point on a wavefront acts as a primary source of secondary spherical wavelets that spread out in forward directions.

Optical Projection Mechanics

  • Overhead Point LED: Projects high-contrast light through the water tray.
  • Water Wave Lenses: Crests focus light like convex lenses; troughs diffuse light like concave lenses.
  • 45° Mirror System: Catches the optical pattern below the water tray and reflects it onto the front screen.
  • Frosted Screen: Displays the final single-slit diffraction pattern clearly.
